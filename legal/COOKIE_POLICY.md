## 1. Introduction

SnapCap ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and extension. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and your rights regarding cookie usage.

## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit our website or use our extension. They help us provide you with a better experience by remembering your preferences and understanding how you use our service.

## 3. Types of Cookies We Use

### 3.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the extension to function properly:

- **Local Storage**: Used to save user preferences (e.g., editor settings, capture options)
- **Chrome Storage API**: Used for extension-specific data and settings synchronization
- **Session Storage**: Used for temporary data during active use sessions

### 3.2 Analytics Cookies

We use minimal analytics to improve our service:

- Usage statistics (features used, capture counts)
- Performance data (error logs, crash reports)
- Device information (browser type, operating system)

### 3.3 Third-Party Cookies

Our extension interacts with the following third-party services that may set cookies:

- **Firebase Authentication**: Session management for user accounts (optional cloud sync)
- **Google Fonts**: Font loading for extension UI (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com)

## 4. Cookie Management

### 4.1 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

- View what cookies are stored on your device
- Delete existing cookies
- Block cookies from specific websites
- Block all cookies (may affect functionality)

### 4.2 Extension Settings

Within the SnapCap extension, you can:

- Clear your local data and preferences
- Manage storage quota usage
- Disable optional cloud sync features

### 4.3 Chrome Storage

To manage Chrome extension storage:

1. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions/`
2. Find SnapCap in the list
3. Click "Details"
4. Scroll to "Site settings" or "Storage" to view/clear data

## 5. Legal Basis for Cookie Usage (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, our use of cookies is based on:

- **Legitimate Interest**: For essential cookies required for the extension to function
- **Consent**: For optional analytics and third-party cookies where applicable

## 6. Your Rights (EEA Users)

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

- Be informed about cookie usage
- Access information about cookies stored on your device
- Object to non-essential cookie usage
- Withdraw consent at any time
