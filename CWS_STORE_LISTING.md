## Detailed Description (CWS Markdown)

**SnapCap Pro** is a 100% free, open-source screenshot and screen recording studio for Google Chrome. Say goodbye to paid subscriptions, watermarks, and paywalled full-page capture features.

### 🚀 Everything You Need To Capture & Communicate
- **Full Page Screenshot**: Seamless scrolling capture of entire long-form articles, landing pages, and web applications.
- **Visible Area & Crop Snip**: Instantly capture the visible viewport or drag-select an exact coordinate box.
- **30-Second Video Clips**: Record short, high-fidelity WebM video walkthroughs with optional tab and microphone audio.
- **Built-in Annotation Studio**:
  - Arrows, shapes, numbers, step indicators
  - Text notes and Callouts
  - Privacy blur & redaction brush for sensitive info
  - Color picker and stroke controls
- **Instant Sharing**: One-click copy image to clipboard (`Ctrl+V` into Slack, Discord, Gmail, GitHub) or direct PNG download.
- **100% Private**: Zero analytics, zero hidden telemetry, zero mandatory cloud account.
